# InstaJam Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

InstaJam ("the app") is a guitar instrument app made by Jesign ("we").

## Information we collect

None. InstaJam does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

- The app does not require an account.
- The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
- The app does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, location, or files.

## Information stored on your device

Your settings (for example chord sets, custom chord sets, tuning, capo, and display options) and the songs you add
are saved only on your device so the app remembers them. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Chord charts

The app downloads its list of chord charts and chart updates from GitHub Pages (jesign.github.io). Like any website,
GitHub receives your device's IP address to deliver these files; no other information is sent. See the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## YouTube

If you link a YouTube video to a song, the app plays it with YouTube's embedded player, which uses YouTube API
Services. The video is loaded directly from YouTube, and YouTube may collect information as described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). By using this feature you agree to the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). We do not receive any of this information.

## Children

InstaJam is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that collect data (for example anonymous usage statistics or ads), we will
update this policy and the app's store listing before that version is released. The effective date above will
change.
