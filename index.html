Features

A real-feeling guitar in your pocket

Every string sounds the moment your finger crosses it, so strums feel fast and natural – like the real thing.

Play instantly

  • Built for instant response – sound starts the moment you touch
  • Chord buttons and strum strings side by side, in landscape
  • Change chords mid-strum with two hands
  • Chuck button for percussive strums and beats
  • Acoustic, classical and clean electric sounds

Play along with songs

  • Browse 90 chord charts: OPM, pop, rock, love songs and worship
  • Opening a song loads its chords onto your chord buttons
  • Auto-scroll at your own speed, with transpose and capo
  • Add your own songs – saved only on your phone
  • Link a YouTube video and strum along with it

Make it yours

  • Ready-made chord sets by key: G, C, D, A and Minor / ballad
  • Up to 3 custom chord sets from 84 chords – drag to reorder
  • Capo on frets 1–12
  • Standard, half-step, whole-step and Drop D tunings, plus fine tuning
  • Left-handed mode and string order options

Private and simple

  • No account, no ads, no tracking
  • Your songs and settings stay on your device
  • Chord charts open offline
